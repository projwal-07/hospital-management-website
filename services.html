Departments and services

Everyday and specialist care

Our departments work together while protecting patient privacy and providing clear information.

Clinical departments

Contact reception if you are unsure which service is suitable. A staff member can explain the general booking process.

Emergency Department

24-hour assessment and stabilisation for urgent illness and injury.

General Medicine

Diagnosis and treatment of common adult medical conditions.

Cardiology

Heart health consultations, monitoring and diagnostic testing.

Paediatrics

Family-centred care for babies, children and teenagers.

Orthopaedics

Treatment and rehabilitation for bone, joint and mobility conditions.

Medical Imaging

X-ray, ultrasound and other imaging services supporting diagnosis.

Pathology

Laboratory testing requested by authorised healthcare professionals.

Physiotherapy

Individual recovery plans supporting movement, strength and independence.

Outpatient Clinics

Scheduled specialist consultations and follow-up appointments.

For a life-threatening emergency

Call Triple Zero (000) immediately. Do not use the website appointment form for urgent medical help.
