EVERGREEN COMMUNITY HOSPITAL - ICT726 WEBSITE NOTES

SITE STRUCTURE
- index.html: creative homepage, business title, 42-word introduction, calls to action and quick information.
- about.html: mission, vision, history and organisational values.
- services.html: nine hospital departments and emergency guidance.
- testimonials.html: static prototype with clearly identified fictional patient stories.
- gallery.html: five photographs and an interactive JavaScript lightbox.
- contact.html: contact details, social links and a validated appointment request form.

TECHNICAL FEATURES
- HTML5 semantic structure including header, nav, main, section, article, aside and footer.
- One shared external stylesheet and one shared JavaScript file.
- Mobile-first media queries for phone, tablet and desktop layouts.
- CSS3 transitions, transforms, rounded corners, opacity, shadows and a gradient image overlay.
- HTML5 and JavaScript form validation with success and error feedback.
- Keyboard-accessible gallery; Escape closes the viewer and focus returns to the selected thumbnail.
- Alt text on all images, ARIA labels, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support.
- No framework, template, PHP, ASP, jQuery or server-side scripting.

PRIVACY AND ETHICS
- The form is a demonstration and does not transmit or store data.
- Hospital details, patient stories and contact information are fictional.
- All photographs are free-to-use stock media. Full links are recorded in images/IMAGE-SOURCES.txt.
- No AI-generated image is included.
