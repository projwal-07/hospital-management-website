About Evergreen

A community hospital with a clear purpose

Our hospital was created to make dependable healthcare easier to reach and easier to understand.

Our mission

To provide safe and compassionate healthcare that respects each patient's circumstances, culture and right to make informed decisions.

Our vision

To be a trusted community hospital known for clear communication, responsible treatment and coordinated support.

Our story

Growing with Western Sydney

Evergreen began in 2011 as a small family medical centre. As the surrounding community grew, the organisation added imaging, specialist clinics, rehabilitation and a 24-hour emergency department.

Today, the hospital remains focused on practical local needs rather than becoming complicated or impersonal.

Values we work by

  • Respect: We listen without judgement.
  • Safety: We follow reliable clinical processes.
  • Honesty: We explain options and limitations clearly.
  • Teamwork: We share information responsibly.
